Privacy Policy — Little Feelings

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Little Feelings is a story app for children ages 3 to 6, made to be used together with a grown-up. This policy explains, in simple terms, how we handle information.

1. We don’t collect personal data

The app doesn’t ask for sign-up, login, name, email, age, or any other personal information, from either the child or the grown-up.

2. What is saved on the device

Reading progress (which page the child stopped on and which stories they finished), the Parents’ area settings (text size, the app language, and whether pages turn automatically when the narration ends), and whether the stories have been unlocked are saved only on the device itself. This information is not sent to us or to anyone else. If the device makes a backup to the grown-up’s account, such as Google or iCloud, this information may be included in that backup, which belongs only to the grown-up. Deleting the app deletes this information from the device.

3. No ads and no tracking

The app doesn’t show ads, doesn’t use analytics tools, and doesn’t track the child or the grown-up.

4. Purchases

Stories are unlocked through the device’s app store (Google Play or the App Store). Payment is processed by the store itself, under its own privacy policy. We don’t receive or store any payment information. The purchase area is protected by a parental gate.

5. Links outside the app

The links to this policy, to the contact email, and to the app’s store page are in the Parents’ area, protected by the same parental gate.

6. Children

The app was made for children and is built with that in mind: we don’t collect data from children, and we follow the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), Brazil’s General Data Protection Law (LGPD), and the app store rules for kids’ apps.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with an updated date.

8. Contact

Questions? Write to bavosistudio@gmail.com.
